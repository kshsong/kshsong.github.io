Professional and Academic Experience

Postdoctoral Researcher | School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Chongqing University | Jul 2024 – Jun 2026

Co-supervisor: Prof. Jun Li. My work covered high-accuracy potential energy surfaces for gas-phase small-molecule reactions, machine learning potential energy surfaces and molecular reaction dynamics, including high-level theoretical calculations and dynamics simulations of the OH + CH3OH and OH + CH3CN systems, as well as the independent development of programs for potential energy surface data sampling, PIP generation and machine learning potential energy surface evaluation.

During the postdoctoral appointment I published 4 SCI-indexed papers and 1 paper in a Chinese-language core journal, including 3 as first or co-first author and 1 as first corresponding author. I participated in one NSFC International (Regional) Cooperation and Exchange Program (1.5 million CNY; listed fourth among the project participants). I also assisted in supervising master’s students, organized academic exchanges, and supported research coordination and day-to-day management of the research group.

CSC-Sponsored Joint Doctoral Training | University of Basel, Switzerland | Jan 2023 – Dec 2023

Advisor: Prof. Markus Meuwly. Funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC) for joint training at the University of Basel, I worked in Prof. Markus Meuwly’s group on machine learning potential energy surfaces, molecular reaction dynamics and machine learning molecular simulations, and published 2 academic papers.
