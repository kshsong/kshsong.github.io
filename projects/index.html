Selected Research Work

The OH + CH3OH reaction system: full-dimensional high-accuracy potential energy surfaces and reaction dynamics

A Δ-Machine Learning approach was used to construct a full-dimensional, high-accuracy analytical neural network potential energy surface, reducing computational cost by 90%. Quasi-classical trajectory simulations were used to study reaction rate coefficients, integral and differential reaction cross sections, and product energy disposal. Normal mode analysis (NMA) combined with quasi-classical dynamics was used to investigate mode-specific reaction dynamics under different vibrational and rotational excitations.

Papers cited in the CV: 1, 3, 4, 7.

Vibrationally induced unimolecular reaction dynamics of H2COO

PhysNet full-dimensional, high-accuracy analytical potential energy surfaces were constructed for two reaction channels of H2COO. Reaction dynamics simulations under vibrationally induced excitation were carried out to analyze the dynamical behavior. The study revealed how vibrational excitation affects the reaction channels and the dynamics, and explored the possibility of deliberately controlling the reaction process.

Paper cited in the CV: 5.

The H2 + NH2− system: full-dimensional potential energy surfaces and reaction dynamics

A PIP-NN full-dimensional, high-accuracy neural network potential energy surface containing three reaction channels was constructed. Quasi-classical dynamics calculations were used to study hydrogen-abstraction rate coefficients and isotope effects. The study found that rotational excitation of H2 inhibits the reaction and analyzed the underlying dynamical mechanism.

Paper cited in the CV: 8.

Research Software and Tools

DML — Efficient sampling program for Δ-Machine Learning

github.com/kshsong/DML

Designed for PIP-NN / FI-NN, it uses the nonlinearity and limited extrapolation ability of neural networks for efficient sampling; automatically removes structurally similar data points to lower the cost of high-level electronic structure calculations; and supports OpenMP parallelism for large-scale sampling.

PIPNN-gen — Automatic generator of PIPs and their Fortran calling code

github.com/kshsong/PIPNN-gen

Automatically generates permutation invariant polynomials (PIPs) for a specified chemical system together with the corresponding Fortran calling code; supports structure screening and parallel removal of similar structures.

h3s-fit — A CQPES-based example of PIP-NN potential energy surface construction and simulation

github.com/kshsong/h3s-fit

Uses the H + H2S system to demonstrate the CQPES and PIP-NN potential energy surface construction workflow; implements PIP-NN training and evaluation, and connects the PIP-NN potential to ASE for single-point energies, geometry optimization and frequency calculations.
